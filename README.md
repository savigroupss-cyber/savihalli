Explain

Instead of refrigeration,

our ancestors preserved ghee in earthen pots.

Clay naturally helps regulate temperature and protects the aroma of the ghee without relying on artificial cold storage.

Our traditional storage method is inspired by these age-old practices to preserve authenticity.

Avoid making medical claims such as "refrigeration is harmful to health" unless you have strong scientific evidence. It is safer to emphasize your traditional process, natural storage, and flavour preservation instead. (Afforise)

The brand positioning

Don't sell it as "just another ghee."

Sell it as:

Savi Halli Thuppa is a celebration of Karnataka's village heritage—crafted from pure cow milk, prepared using traditional methods, and inspired by the way generations preserved and cherished ghee before modern conveniences. Every jar carries the aroma, taste, and warmth of an authentic village kitchen.

I would also recommend refining the brand name slightly

Instead of displaying:

savi_halli_thuppa

Use this on the website:

SAVI HALLI THUPPA

Pure Cow Ghee from the Heart of Karnataka

The underscore (_) is suitable for social media handles, but on your packaging and website, using spaces creates a more premium and timeless appearance.

Based on the quality of your label and your story, this brand has the potential to stand alongside premium artisanal food brands if the website focuses on authenticity, craftsmanship, and transparency, rather than competing on price alone. Customers in the premium food category consistently respond well to detailed origin stories, production videos, and clear explanations of what makes the product special. (Legacy Food)
